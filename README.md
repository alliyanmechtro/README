# README
What ICT Means to Me

ICT means Information and Communication Technology. Before studying this subject, I did not know much about ICT. I mostly thought it was just about computers and the internet.

After learning a little about it, I understand that ICT is used for many things in our daily life. We use technology to communicate with other people, find information, study, share files, and do different tasks more easily.

As a university student, I use my phone and laptop for studying, making assignments, searching for information, and communicating with my classmates and teachers. I am also learning how tools like GitHub can be used to keep and share our work.

I am still new to ICT, so I have a lot more to learn. I hope this course will help me understand technology better and learn skills that will be useful in my studies and future career
